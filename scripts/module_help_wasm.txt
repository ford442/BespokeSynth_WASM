# WASM module help supplement (English), baked by scripts/bake_module_help.py.
# Same format as resource/tooltips_eng.txt: "type~description", then "~control~tooltip".
# Keyed by WASM type ids and control names (the kParams names). Lines here win over the
# desktop text, so a module that behaves differently in the browser says so here.
# "@wasmtype=desktoptype" borrows a desktop entry when only the type id differs.

@filter=biquad
@reverb=freeverb

adsr~shapes the audio passing through with an attack/decay/sustain/release envelope, opened by input notes
~attack~time to rise to full level after a note-on, in milliseconds
~decay~time to fall from full level to the sustain level, in milliseconds
~sustain~level held while the note is down
~release~time to fade out after the note-off, in milliseconds

arpeggiator~arpeggiates held notes in step with the transport
~rate~step length of the arpeggio
~mode~order to play the held notes in: up, down, up/down, random, or as played
~octaves~how many octaves the pattern climbs before it starts over
~gate~how long each arpeggiated note sounds, as a fraction of a step

bitcrush~reduce sample resolution and sample rate for lo-fi effects
~mix~blend between the clean input and the crushed signal

chorder~takes an incoming pitch and plays additional notes to form chords
~chord~chord shape to build on each incoming note
~inversion~move the lowest chord tones up an octave
~tone2~custom chord: semitones above the root of the second note
~tone3~custom chord: semitones above the root of the third note
~tone4~custom chord: semitones above the root of the fourth note
~level~velocity of the added chord tones relative to the played note

chorus~two modulated delay voices for width and shimmer
~rate~speed of the delay modulation, in Hz
~depth~amount of delay modulation
~delay~centre delay time of the voices, in milliseconds
~feedback~amount of each voice fed back into itself
~mix~blend between the dry input and the chorused signal
~width~spread of the two voices across left and right

clockdivider~passes every Nth incoming pulse
~divide~let one pulse through out of this many
~offset~which pulse in each group gets through

compressor~try to keep volume at a certain level
~makeup~gain added after compression, in dB
~knee~width of the soft transition around the threshold, in dB

delay~echoing delay
~time~delay time, in seconds
~mix~blend between the dry input and the echoes
~pingpong~bounce the echoes between left and right

distortion~waveshaping distortion
~level~output volume after the distortion

drumseq~four-track drum step sequencer, clocked by pulses. each track has its own note output
~kick~kick track steps (click the grid)
~snare~snare track steps (click the grid)
~hat~hi-hat track steps (click the grid)
~tom~tom track steps (click the grid)
~kickpitch~note the kick track plays
~snarepitch~note the snare track plays
~hatpitch~note the hi-hat track plays
~tompitch~note the tom track plays
~steps~length of the pattern, in steps
~gate~how long each hit's note lasts, as a fraction of a step

envelope~an ADSR envelope as a modulation output. a note-on starts it, the last note-off releases it, and a pulse fires a one-shot
~attack~time to rise from low to high, in milliseconds
~decay~time to fall to the sustain level, in milliseconds
~sustain~level held while a note is down, as a fraction of the way from low to high
~release~time to return to low after the note-off, in milliseconds
~low~output when the envelope is closed
~high~output at the envelope's peak
~useVelocity~scale the peak by note velocity or pulse strength

eq~four-band equalizer (low shelf, two peaks, high shelf). the curve shows the combined response
~lowFreq~corner frequency of the low shelf
~lowGain~boost or cut of the low shelf, in dB
~midFreq~centre frequency of the first peak band
~midGain~boost or cut of the first peak band, in dB
~midQ~width of the first peak band (higher is narrower)
~hiMidFreq~centre frequency of the second peak band
~hiMidGain~boost or cut of the second peak band, in dB
~hiMidQ~width of the second peak band (higher is narrower)
~highFreq~corner frequency of the high shelf
~highGain~boost or cut of the high shelf, in dB
~output~output trim after the bands, in dB

fmsynth~polyphonic fm synthesis, up to eight voices (the oldest note is stolen past that)
~attack~volume envelope attack, in milliseconds
~decay~volume envelope decay, in milliseconds
~sustain~volume envelope sustain level
~release~volume envelope release, in milliseconds
~modAttack~modulation index envelope attack, in milliseconds
~modDecay~modulation index envelope decay, in milliseconds
~modSustain~modulation index held while the note is down, as a fraction of mod
~voices~maximum number of notes that sound at once

filter~resonant filter. the CV input sweeps the cutoff up to two octaves either way
~cutoff~cutoff frequency, in Hz
~resonance~emphasis at the cutoff frequency
~type~filter response: lowpass, highpass, bandpass or notch

input~get audio from input source, like a microphone
~gain~input level
~mute~silence the input without closing the microphone

karplusstrong~polyphonic plucked string physical modeling synth
~source~audio to use for excitation: sine, noise, a pitch-dependent mix, or saw
~release~how quickly the string is damped after the note-off, in milliseconds
~voices~maximum number of strings that sound at once

lfo~low-frequency oscillator as a modulation output. cable it to a CV, Mod or Drive input
~rate~speed of the oscillation, in Hz
~depth~amount of modulation
~shape~waveform: sine, saw, square or triangle

looper~loop input audio, in time with the transport
~mix~level of the loop against the live input
~bars~length of the loop, in bars
~command~record, overdub, play, stop or clear the loop

mixer~four-channel mixer with pan
~level1~volume of channel 1
~level2~volume of channel 2
~level3~volume of channel 3
~level4~volume of channel 4
~pan1~stereo position of channel 1
~pan2~stereo position of channel 2
~pan3~stereo position of channel 3
~pan4~stereo position of channel 4
~master~output volume

noise~noise generator
~volume~output volume
~color~tone of the noise, from white (bright) to dark

notechance~randomly allow notes through
~seed~starting point of the random sequence; the same seed repeats the same pattern

notefilter~only allow a range of pitches through
~low~lowest pitch of the range
~high~highest pitch of the range
~mode~pass the notes inside the range, or the ones outside it

noterouter~sends notes to one of several outputs
~outputs~number of outputs in use
~mode~round robin (each note to the next output) or pitch split (by key range)
~split~in pitch split mode, the lowest note of the second output's range
~zone~in pitch split mode, the width of each output's key range, in semitones

notesequencer~looping sequence of notes on a grid: click a cell to set a step's pitch, drag the lanes below for velocity and length
~steps~length of the sequence, in steps
~interval~step length
~octave~octave of the grid's bottom row
~rows~number of pitch rows in the grid
~mode~rows step through the Scale's degrees, or through semitones

oscillator~enveloped oscillator, played by notes (it drones on its own frequency when no note cable is connected)
~frequency~pitch when no notes are playing it, in Hz
~volume~output volume
~waveform~waveform: sine, saw, square or triangle

output~route audio in here to send it to your speakers
~level~master volume
~limit~catch peaks with a limiter before they clip
~dcblock~remove DC offset from the output

panner~pan audio left and right. also, converts a mono input to a stereo output.
~gain~output volume

pingpongdelay~stereo delay whose echoes alternate left and right, synced to the transport
~interval~delay time as a note length, or "ms" to use the time slider
~delay~delay time when the interval is set to ms, in milliseconds
~feedback~how much of each echo comes back as the next one
~mix~blend between the dry input and the echoes
~tone~lowpass on the echoes; each repeat gets a little darker
~width~how far apart the echoes are panned

pitchbender~add pitch bend to notes
~range~semitones of bend at full deflection

pulsetonote~turns pulses into notes
~pitch~note to play on each pulse
~gate~length of each note, in beats

ramper~a ramp as a modulation output: a note or pulse ramps from start to target over the length, or loop it in time with the transport
~start~value the ramp starts from
~target~value the ramp ends at
~length~time the ramp takes
~mode~one-shot (ramp when triggered, then hold) or loop (a tempo-synced sawtooth)

reverb~reverb using the "freeverb" algorithm
~room~size of the room
~damping~how quickly high frequencies die away in the tail
~mix~blend between the dry input and the reverb

sampler~polyphonic sample player. drop an audio file on the canvas to load it
~volume~output volume
~start~where playback starts in the sample
~end~where playback ends in the sample
~loopStart~loop start point, in loop mode
~loopEnd~loop end point, in loop mode
~mode~one-shot, loop, or gate (plays while the note is held)
~root~the note that plays the sample at its original pitch

scale~controls the global scale used by various modules
~type~scale type: major, minor, dorian, mixolydian, pentatonic, blues or chromatic

seaofgrain~granular synth: each note sprays grains of the sample. drop an audio file on the canvas to load one
~mode~whether keys set the grain pitch or the position in the sample
~root~in pitch mode, the key that plays the grains at original speed
~attack~fade-in of each note's grain cloud, in milliseconds
~release~fade-out after the note-off, in milliseconds
~voices~maximum number of notes that sound at once
~volume~output volume
~pos~where in the sample the grains come from
~overlap~number of overlapping grains
~lenMs~length of each grain in milliseconds
~posRandom~randomization of grain start point, in milliseconds
~speedRandom~randomization of grain speed
~spacingRandom~randomization of time between grains
~octaves~add grains a fifth up and a fourth down
~width~stereo spread of the grains
~speed~grain playback speed
~keyboardBasePitch~in position mode, the key that maps to the position slider
~keyboardNumPitches~in position mode, how many keys span the whole sample

send~duplicate a signal and send it to a second destination
~amount~level of the signal sent to the Send output
~crossfade~fade the main output down as the send goes up

stepsequencer~sixteen-step gate sequencer that plays one note
~pattern~the steps that play (click the grid)
~pitch~note every step plays
~gate~how long each step's note lasts, as a fraction of a step
~steps~length of the pattern, in steps

stereowidth~widen or narrow the stereo image
~width~0 folds to mono, 1 leaves the input as it is, 2 exaggerates the sides
~widen~delay on the right channel (the Haas effect), which widens even a mono source, in milliseconds
~level~output volume

transport~controls tempo and current time position
~bpm~tempo in beats per minute

vocoder~FFT vocoder: the Carrier input (a synth) takes on the spectral envelope of the Modulator input (a voice). one frame of latency
~input~level of the modulator input
~carrier~level of the carrier input
~volume~output volume
~mix~blend between the dry carrier and the vocoded signal
~bands~number of frequency bands the envelope is measured in
~smooth~how slowly the band envelopes follow the modulator

waveshaper~waveshaping distortion with a selectable curve. the Drive input modulates the drive
~drive~amount of gain into the curve
~mode~curve: soft, tanh, fold or hard clip
~mix~blend between the dry input and the shaped signal
~level~output volume
